Learning AI Through Its Mathematics

HS-Class runs hands-on workshops that teach the mathematics behind artificial intelligence and machine learning. Learners observe data, spot patterns, generalize, and build their own simple models.

İYTE Teknopark, İzmir Ages 9+ Small-group workshops
Visit hs-class.com Get in Touch

How It Works

In-Person Workshops

Small, applied groups where every learner works through the ideas by hand.

Interactive Exercises

Mini-apps and browser-based Python exercises, with nothing to install.

Integral Loop

A feedback system that measures progress and shares personalized roadmaps with learners, instructors, and parents.

Instructor-Approved

AI-assisted feedback is always reviewed and approved by an instructor.

Age Groups

9–13

A first, intuitive look at data, patterns, and models.

13–17

Deeper models and the mathematics that make them work.

17+

A rigorous path from the foundations to deep learning.

Curriculum & Vision

The goal is a solid mathematical foundation for AI. The first workshops cover core machine-learning tasks, and mathematics workshops come next.

  • Classification
  • Recommendation
  • Regression
  • Basic Deep Learning
  • Calculus
  • Linear Algebra
  • Probability & Statistics
